FADE IN:

INT. MARA'S APARTMENT - NIGHT

A cramped one-bedroom, cluttered with unopened moving boxes. Rain streaks the window. MARA (30s, exhausted in the specific way of someone who hasn't slept in a different reality) stares at a radio on the kitchen table. It isn't plugged in. It hums anyway.

MARA

(to the radio)

I know you can hear me. I checked the relay logs. Someone on this line has been listening for six weeks.

The radio's dial creeps, on its own, from one frequency to another. Static resolves into something almost like a voice.

RADIO VOICE (V.O.)

(warped, layered - like two people speaking a half-second apart)

Six weeks here too. Give or take a Tuesday.

Mara sits down hard, like her legs stopped consulting her.

MARA

You're not supposed to be this clear. Relay coverage out here is garbage.

RADIO VOICE (V.O.)

Coverage isn't the problem. You are. You keep almost hanging up.

A long beat. Mara reaches for the dial, then stops herself.

MARA

(quietly)

What do I call you? "Me" feels wrong.

RADIO VOICE (V.O.)

Call me Tuesday. It's been a long week over here.

CUT TO:

EXT. RELAY ARRAY - CONTINUOUS

Rain hammers a chain-link fence around a squat concrete building bristling with antennae. A single light glows in a ground-floor window. Nobody is there to see it.

FADE OUT.
