## **1. Opening Scene**

In a sunlit New York bedroom on a quiet afternoon, a young woman moves through a familiar, intimate space—music playing softly, light shifting across objects that suggest both creativity and introspection.

---

## **2. Introduction of the Protagonist**

Mila, 22, is thoughtful, curious, and slightly untethered—someone who believes there is more to reality than what's immediately visible. She uses an app called *Parallel*, which allows her to communicate with alternate versions of herself. Among them, she feels especially connected to one: Mila #8, a version of herself traveling abroad in search of family history and meaning.

---

## **3. Inciting Incident**

While casually navigating the app, Mila checks Mila #8's messages—still no response after weeks. She drifts through other parts of the app, briefly connecting with another version of herself, Robbie, toward whom she feels an unexpected attraction. Just as she prepares to revisit a saved dream, Mila #8 finally reappears with a message, reigniting Mila's attention and curiosity.

---

## **4. First Turning Point**

Their exchange is brief—Mila responds eagerly, but Mila #8 disappears again mid-conversation. Left in a suspended state, Mila disengages from the app momentarily, moving through her room, restless and unfocused.

---

## **5. A New Life**

Returning to the app, Mila discovers a new beta feature: *Echoes*. Intrigued, she prepares herself—silencing the room, dimming the lights, lying still. Despite a skeptical text from her sister dismissing the app as artificial or delusional, Mila commits to the experience.

---

## **6. Midpoint Shift**

Through a guided induction, Mila enters a trance and witnesses a vivid scene from what appears to be a past life: a 1930s photography studio where she exists as another version of herself. In an intimate moment with a lover, she is unexpectedly captured in a photograph—seen, despite her resistance.

---

## **7. False Hope and Setback**

Mila awakens disoriented, hours having passed. She frantically records the experience, trying to hold onto its sensory reality. Just as she begins to process it, Mila #8 sends a collection of old family photographs she has discovered abroad.

---

## **8. Darkest Moment**

As Mila scrolls through the images, subtle similarities begin to emerge—same textures, same light, same space. Then she reaches the final photo: the exact moment she just experienced in her vision. The realization destabilizes her completely.

---

## **9. Climactic Showdown**

Before she can respond, Mila receives a *Parallel Exchange Request* from Mila #8—a chance to temporarily inhabit each other's lives. The invitation is casual, almost playful, but for Mila, it lands with overwhelming weight in the wake of what she's just witnessed.

---

## **10. Resolution**

Mila hesitates, beginning to respond—then stopping. Faced with the possibility that everything she believed might be real, she makes a quiet, irreversible decision.

She accepts.

---

## **11. Closing Scene**

A countdown begins.

Mila sits motionless, staring at the screen as the moment approaches.

Cut to black.
