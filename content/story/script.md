# PARALLEL

Written by Mary Stanton

INT. MILA’S BEDROOM — LATE AFTERNOON

A still room.

Soft summer light filters through open curtains. The breeze through the window is slow. Pothos leaves moving slightly. Dust particles linger.

A lava lamp glob shifts on a shelf. Low music crackles faintly from a radio.

The door opens.

MILA (22) enters, casual—sweatpants, oversized tee, slightly disheveled. A little messy, unbothered by the day.

She drops a tote bag near the door.

Crosses to the radio.

Turns the dial—static, fragments of songs—until she finds something she likes.

Moves to her bed. Flops onto it.

Stares at the ceiling.

A beat.

She reaches for her phone.

PHONE — PARALLEL APP

Already open.

MILA

Taps directly into a thread.

CHAT — MILA #8

95% MATCH

Profile photo in the header: sun, coastline, old stone buildings.

Mila scrolls.

Fragments of conversation:

* travel
* family
* “I think I’m getting closer to something”

She reaches the bottom.

No new messages.

She pulls down.

Refresh.

Nothing.

A small exhale.

BACK — PARALLELS

She scrolls.

Stops on another profile.

ROBBIE — Parallel 22

A trans version of herself. Different name. Different energy.

Confident. Soft. Attractive.

Mila lingers then taps.

CHAT — ROBBIE

Photos shared between them.

A recent message from Robbie—light, slightly flirty.

Mila reads it again.

She taps the video icon.

Calling…

CALL DECLINED

A message appears:

“sorry, can’t do video right now. it’s 2am here. i’m in bed”

Mila stares at it.

A beat.

“Okay. Goodnight”

Not upset, but a little deflated.

She exits.

DREAMS TAB

A different interface.

Saved dreams.

Some tagged. Some starred.

She scrolls.

Stops on one:

“Robbie sex dream by the river”

❤️ Favorited

Revisited: 3 times

She taps it.

She’s about to revisit the dream and then

NOTIFICATION — MILA #8

Mila freezes.

Immediately taps.

CHAT — MILA #8

New message:

“sorry i disappeared for a bit, been moving around a lot but i’ve been thinking about you!”

Mila lights up.

She types quickly.

“girl it’s okay! i’ve been thinking about you too. what did you find?”

She sends.

Typing dots appear.

Mila watches.

They stop.

No message.

She waits a second longer.

Nothing.

Mila leans back slowly.

INT. BEDROOM — CONTINUOUS

She sets the phone down.

Stares up again.

A small exhale.

Not dramatic. Just… suspended.

She sits up.

Grabs a watering can.

Moves around the room—watering plants.

The music continues softly.

She pauses mid-motion.

Thinking.

She sets the watering can down.

Returns to the bed.

Picks up her phone again.

PHONE — PARALLEL

A new in-app prompt.

ECHOES — BETA AVAILABLE

Mila tilts her head slightly.

Taps.

ECHOES SCREEN (GLIMPSED)

Text scrolls softly:

“A guided experience to access memory beyond your current lifetime—visual + tonal induction required”

Preparation steps flicker past:

“Dim the lights.”
“Find a quiet space.”

Mila scans them. Not overthinking it.

INT. BEDROOM — CONTINUOUS

She stands.

Turns off the radio.

The room goes quiet.

She dims the lights down to 0.

Now just ambient daylight remains. She closes the curtains and a tiny bit seeps through.

Her phone BUZZES.

A regular text.

PHONE — TEXT MESSAGE

SISTER

“come out tonight??”

Mila types:

“can’t, busy”

Typing bubble appears.

SISTER:

“lol are you talking to your weird parallel selves again”

Mila smirks slightly.

Types:

“fuck off”

Sends.

SISTER hearts the message.

Puts the phone down.

BACK TO ECHOES

She lies on her side, back against the wall.

Props the phone nearby, screen angled toward her.

Takes a breath.

Taps:

BEGIN

ECHOES — INDUCTION

CALM VOICE

“Settle in. Allow your body to relax.”

Mila closes her eyes.

The screen reads:

“Settle into a comfortable position.
Allow the room to remain dark and quiet.
The camera flash and tones will help guide you into a meditative state.”

A pause.

VOICE (CONT’D)

I’ll ask you a few questions.

Mila responds softly (barely audible).

The voice continues—but begins to blur.

Words dissolve into tones.

Low, layered, resonant.

Something choral. Vibrational.

Light flickers faintly across her eyelids.

Then—

VISION — 1930s PHOTOGRAPHY STUDIO

Dust in sunlight.

Wood floors. Quiet, still space.

Mila—another version—adjusts a large camera, sure-handed, at ease in the space.

A lover nearby. Masculine-presenting. Warm. Playful.

They move easily around each other, like this has happened a hundred times.

A moment of stillness.

The lover watches her. Smiles. Steps closer.

Encouraging.

They laugh together.

The lover pulls her gently into frame. Close. Too close.

Mila softens.

They look at each other.

The lover casually grips the shutter bulb.

Mila doesn’t notice.

A breath—

CLICK.

The photo is taken.

Mila reacts, playfully shoves them.

They laugh.

Stay close.

The moment lingers.

INT. BEDROOM — NIGHT

Mila wakes sharply.

Sits up.

Disoriented.

The room is dark now.

She grabs her phone.

10:47 PM

She blinks.

Processing.

She scrambles for her journal.

Opens it.

Starts writing fast.

Then tosses the notebook and grabs her phone instead.

Parallel app is still opened. She taps voice-to-text in the post-Echoes debrief screen.

Speaking as she writes:

“there was dust in the air, it smelled metallic, like chemicals. he tricked me into the photo, i didn’t want it but i didn’t mind. it felt like love and safety. i knew all this stuff about my life, but it was different — i was different.”

She pauses.

Breathing.

Her phone BUZZES.

CHAT — MILA #8

“I found something today… a box of old photos of my family that goes way back.”

Another message.

“there are so many of them! they’re kinda amazing”

Then—

A photo batch appears.

PHOTO GALLERY

Mila opens it.

Starts swiping.

Photo 1 — old family portrait

Photo 2 — landscape

Photo 3 — street

Photo 4 — interior

She keeps going.

Photo 6 — something familiar in the lighting

Photo 7 — a studio corner

Photo 8 — same era, same textures

She slows down.

Photo 9 — closer… similar framing

Then—

Photo 10.

She stops.

Zooms slightly.

It’s the moment.

The same composition.

The same closeness.

The same pose.

MILA

Still.
Silent.
Trying to process.

Her hand tightens around the phone.

Another notification.

CHAT — MILA #8

“pretty cool right? i think she’s my great grandmother. there are like hundreds more but these are my faves”

Then a new card appears in the chat.

PARALLEL EXCHANGE REQUEST

Mila #8 has requested a temporary exchange

Duration: 15 minutes

Buttons:

Decline / Accept

A message appears under it:

“i’ve never tried this before but i’d love to see new york, even for a few minutes. I could check out that bagel place you told me about by your apt?”

Mila stares.

Still holding the phone.

Everything stacked at once.

The vision.
The photos.
The request.

She starts typing.

Stops.

Deletes.

Her thumb hovers over Decline.

A beat.

She moves her thumb.

Taps—

ACCEPT

The screen shifts:

Exchange initializing
Prepare for synchronization
00:59

Mila doesn’t move.

Just watches.

CUT TO BLACK.

END
