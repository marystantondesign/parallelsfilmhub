# PARALLEL

Written by Mary Stanton

INT. MILA’S BEDROOM — LATE AFTERNOON

A still room.

Soft summer light filters through open curtains. The air feels warm, slow.

A lava lamp gently shifts on a shelf. Low music crackles faintly from a radio.

The door opens.

MILA (22) enters, casual—sweatpants, oversized tee, slightly disheveled. Not messy, just unbothered by the day.

She drops a tote bag near the door.

Crosses to the radio.

Turns the dial—static, fragments of songs—until she finds something she likes.

She nods. Leaves it playing.

Moves to her bed. Flops onto it.

Stares at the ceiling.

A beat.

She reaches for her phone.

PHONE — PARALLEL APP

Already open.

Familiar.

MILA

Taps directly into a thread.

CHAT — MILA #8

95% MATCH

Photos in the header: sun, coastline, old stone buildings.

Mila scrolls.

Fragments of conversation:

* travel
* family
* “I think I’m getting closer to something”
* “I wish you were here”

She reaches the bottom.

No new messages.

She pulls down.

Refresh.

Nothing.

A small exhale.

BACK — PARALLELS

She scrolls.

Stops on another profile.

ROBBIE — Parallel #??

A trans version of herself. Different name. Different energy.

Confident. Soft. Attractive.

Mila lingers.

A tiny smile. Almost subconscious.

She taps.

CHAT — ROBBIE

Photos shared between them.

A recent message from Robbie—light, slightly flirty.

Mila reads it again.

She taps the video icon.

Calling…

CALL DECLINED

A message appears:

sorry, can’t right now

Mila stares at it.

A beat.

“Okay.”

Not upset. Just… a little deflated.

She exits.

DREAMS TAB

A different interface.

Saved dreams.

Some tagged. Some starred.

She scrolls.

Stops on one:

“Robbie — river / night”

❤️ Favorited

Revisited: 3 times

She taps it.

A preview—blurry, atmospheric.

She’s about to enter—

NOTIFICATION — MILA #8

Mila freezes.

Immediately taps.

CHAT — MILA #8

New message:

sorry i disappeared for a bit
been moving around a lot
i’ve been thinking about you

Mila lights up—subtle but real.

She types quickly.

no it’s okay
i’ve been thinking about you too
what did you find?

She sends.

Typing dots appear.

Mila watches.

They stop.

No message.

She waits a second longer.

Nothing.

Mila leans back slowly.

INT. BEDROOM — CONTINUOUS

She sets the phone down.

Stares up again.

A small exhale.

Not dramatic. Just… suspended.

She sits up.

Grabs a watering can.

Moves around the room—watering plants.

The music continues softly.

She pauses mid-motion.

Thinking.

She sets the watering can down.

Returns to the bed.

Picks up her phone again.

PHONE — PARALLEL

A new in-app prompt.

ECHOES — BETA AVAILABLE

Mila tilts her head slightly.

Curious.

Taps.

ECHOES SCREEN (GLIMPSED)

Text scrolls softly:

a guided experience
access memory beyond your current lifetime
visual + tonal induction required

Preparation steps flicker past.

Mila scans.

Not overthinking it.

INT. BEDROOM — CONTINUOUS

She stands.

Turns off the radio.

The room goes quiet.

She dims the lights.

Now just ambient daylight remains.

Her phone BUZZES.

A regular text.

PHONE — TEXT MESSAGE

SISTER

come out tonight??

Mila types:

can’t, busy

Typing bubble appears.

SISTER:

lol are you talking to your weird parallel selves again

Mila smirks slightly.

Types:

fuck off

Sends.

Puts the phone down.

BACK TO ECHOES

She lies on her side, back against the wall.

Props the phone nearby, angled toward her.

Takes a breath.

Taps:

BEGIN

ECHOES — INDUCTION

A calm voice.

Not overly human. Not robotic. Just… steady.

VOICE

Settle in.
Allow your body to relax.

Mila closes her eyes.

VOICE (CONT’D)

You don’t need to go anywhere.
You’re already connected.

A pause.

VOICE (CONT’D)

I’ll ask you a few questions.

Mila responds softly (barely audible).

The voice continues—but begins to blur.

Words dissolve into tones.

Low, layered, resonant.

Something choral. Vibrational.

Light flickers faintly across her eyelids.

Then—

VISION — 1930s PHOTOGRAPHY STUDIO

Dust in sunlight.

Wood floors. Quiet space.

Mila—another version—adjusts a large camera.

Focused. Comfortable.

A lover nearby.

Masculine-presenting. Warm. playful.

They move easily around each other.

A moment of stillness.

The lover watches Mila.

Smiles.

Steps closer.

Encouraging.

Mila resists slightly—hides her face.

They laugh.

The lover pulls her gently into frame.

Close.

Too close.

Mila softens.

They look at each other.

The lover casually grips the shutter bulb.

Mila doesn’t notice.

A breath—

CLICK.

The photo is taken.

Mila reacts—playfully shoves them.

They laugh.

Stay close.

The moment lingers.

INT. BEDROOM — NIGHT

Mila wakes sharply.

Sits up.

Disoriented.

The room is dark now.

She grabs her phone.

10:47 PM

She blinks.

Processing.

She scrambles for her journal.

Opens it.

Starts writing fast.

At the same time—

She taps voice-to-text in the app.

Speaking as she writes:

there was dust in the air
it smelled metallic
like chemicals
he tricked me into the photo
i didn’t want it
but i didn’t mind

She pauses.

Breathing.

Her phone BUZZES.

CHAT — MILA #8

i found something today
a box of old photos
i think they’re from my family

Another message.

there are so many of them

Then—

A photo batch appears.

PHOTO GALLERY

Mila opens it.

Starts swiping.

Photo 1 — old family portrait

Photo 2 — landscape

Photo 3 — street

Photo 4 — interior

She keeps going.

Photo 6 — something familiar in the lighting

Photo 7 — a studio corner

Photo 8 — same era, same textures

She slows down.

Photo 9 — closer… similar framing

Then—

Photo 10.

She stops.

Zooms slightly.

It’s the moment.

The same composition.

The same closeness.

The same pose.

MILA

Still.
Silent.
Trying to process.

Her hand tightens around the phone.

Another notification.

CHAT — MILA #8

pretty cool right
i think she’s my great grandmother
there are like hundreds more

Then—

A new card appears in the chat.

PARALLEL EXCHANGE REQUEST

Mila #8 has requested a temporary exchange

Duration: 15 minutes

Buttons:

Decline
Accept

A message appears under it:

i’ve never tried this before
but i’d love to see new york
even just for a few minutes
that bagel place you told me about

Mila stares.

Still holding the phone.

Everything stacked at once.

The vision.
The photos.
The request.

She starts typing.

Stops.

Deletes.

Her thumb hovers over Decline.

A beat.

She moves her thumb.

Taps—

ACCEPT

The screen shifts:

Exchange initializing
Prepare for synchronization
00:59

Mila doesn’t move.

Just watches.

CUT TO BLACK.

END
